### Changed

- **Back to Home from a cover page of a book, and the first Back after the start, are quicker on the X4** (#267). After grays the panel had to be driven whole by a half refresh (1.7 s) that began only once Home was drawn (1.5 s): the X4 now cleans the panel to white while Home is drawn and shows it with a fast refresh (0.5 s; the X4 and X4 Classic's SSD1677 panel only). And Home lays its page out in the minute a book the device opened at its start is read, so the first Back finds it kept (it laid it out then, 1.2 s more than a later Back).
