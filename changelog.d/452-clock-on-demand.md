### Added

- Longer battery life on the X4 and X3: the processor runs at full speed (160 MHz) only while the reader works (a page turn, opening a book, drawing, the radio) and waits at a slow clock in between, and at an even slower one in the Saving and Offline modes (#452). A new power mode, *Performance* (Leistung), keeps the full speed all the time: the quickest, but the battery lasts shorter. Choose it in the quick panel or under Settings, Power. Plugins are not affected.
