### Changed

- **Sliders and round buttons in the settings on touch screens** (#442). Numbers that ask for a slider (front light, light colour, text and picture sizes, margins and more) show the same row as the quick panel again, as in Beta 3: the title, then − value + tight at the right with round buttons, and the track below across almost the whole width, so one swipe sets what took many taps. The knob follows the finger and only its row is drawn again; lifting the finger keeps the number. Numbers without a slider have the round − and + too. Screens with buttons keep the compact row.
