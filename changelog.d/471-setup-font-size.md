### Fixed

- **Setup: the font size could only be left with Back, which also went a page back** (#471). As in Beta 3, Select on the size starts changing it, the arrows change it, Select keeps it and Back only cancels the change; the arrows otherwise move on to Next (setup 1.0.6).
- **Setup: the time zone cycled through every zone on each press** (#471). As in Beta 3, a choice of more than two options opens their list to pick from, and Next has the focus after picking (setup 1.0.6).
- **Font sizes that looked the same were offered** (#471). The reading font has a few pixel sizes, so on the X4 Pro and X4 Classic 9 and 10 pt looked alike. The setup, the settings and the quick menu offer only sizes that look different, as in Beta 3; a size kept from before shows as the one it looks like (setup 1.0.6, settings 1.0.13, menu 1.0.12).
