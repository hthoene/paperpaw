### Changed

- **Home editor: an item's options are the bottom menu** (#450). Tapping or choosing an item while you edit the home screen (or the picture home) opens the same compact menu at the bottom of the screen as everywhere else (Move, Change the size, Settings, Remove) instead of a dialog with large buttons in the middle. Back, or a tap beside the menu, closes it, so the *Cancel* button is gone (home 1.0.17, picture home 1.0.6).
