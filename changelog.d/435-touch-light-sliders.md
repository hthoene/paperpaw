### Changed

- **Bigger sliders and resize handles on touch screens** (#435, #436). In the quick panel the Front light and Light colour sliders (and every other slider in a panel) now have a row of their own across almost the whole width, under the label and the − and + buttons, so a finger hits and moves them easily. When arranging the home screen, the corner that sizes a widget is drawn as two circles again (a ring with a dot) and its touch area is at least a fingertip.
