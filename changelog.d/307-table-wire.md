### Changed

- **The firmware is about 95 KB smaller: records are encoded and decoded from tables** (#307). Every record a plugin and PaperPaw exchange (service calls, events, declarations, the stages of the format, widget, status, lookup, input filter and book badge plugins) had its own generated decoder, sizer and writer, in PaperPaw and again in the plugin kit. Now a record is a table of its fields, and one decoder, one sizer and one writer walk it. The bytes on the wire are the same, byte for byte, so plugins built before keep working unchanged, and the plugin interface did not change. A call costs no more than before: decoding got faster (text that is plain ASCII, as ids and paths are, is checked a word at a time), encoding a little slower, together slightly less work per record. A plugin's own stage schemas (`tools/stage-codec.py`) keep their generated code: with a handful of records it is smaller than a table walker.
