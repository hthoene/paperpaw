### Changed

- **The home screen holds no page while another screen is over it** (#248). The home screen's page (its cells, rows, texts and the widgets made for them) is let go when the screen stack changes and home is no longer on top (a book is read, a settings page is open) and is laid out again when home is shown, as it was at every show. A list of a plugin's open screens reserves room for the one screen it adds instead of four.
