### Added

- **Five more first-party plugins are linked native plugins** (#228, #243). With `native-linked` the list `paperpaw_app::NATIVE_PLUGINS` gains sleep, and with the new feature `native-linked-store` (which the emulator names) aids, mail, notes-vault and widgets; their interface-6 forms are not installed then. `native_registers` says which of them register: the plugins the store offers keep the state they ship in (mail and notes-vault off until `[plugins] id = on`, an `off` in the configuration wins, none where the store installs them as files). Off by default, as `native-linked` is. The formats (txt, xtc, docx, images, mobi), home, reader, dictionary, store, wifi, transfer, fonts-browser, vocabulary, news and picture-home stay interface 6 until the native reader exists and their ports are finished.
