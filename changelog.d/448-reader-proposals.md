### Changed

- **Reader: quicker controls and less covered text** (#448).
  - Holding a page button goes to the next or previous chapter; a short press turns the page as before (the setting *Hold for next chapter*, now on at first).
  - On the X4 the row of button hints (*Back · Select · ‹ ›*) shows again under the reader's menu, as in Beta 3: it was not drawn when the screen before had the same hints.
  - Choosing text shows its actions in a slim bar at the bottom (one row where the labels fit, else two), so the sentence round the word stays readable.
  - A tap on the status line can also show the pages left in the chapter (*Chapter: 6 pages left*) beside the progress.
  - The search list says what was searched for and how many hits there are in its header, and shows the word found in bold in each hit.
  - After a jump, *‹ Back* names the page it returns to (*‹ p. 13*) once the pages are counted.
  - A tap on the chapter's name in the reader's menu opens the table of contents.
  - The font panel keeps to the text: front light and light colour are in the quick panel only.
  - The first-time help of a book shows the tap zones with their names (*Previous page*, *Menu*, *Next page*) beside its box instead of only listing them.
  - The list of bookmarks and highlights is grouped under the chapters of the book.
