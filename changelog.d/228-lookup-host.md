### Added

- **`core.lookup` is served by the host** (#228, `docs/abi/document.md` section 7). The providers the plugins declared (`lookup.spec`) are read from the declarations, left out when they are the asker's own, need a network that is not connected or do not take the ask's language (an empty list or an empty ask takes all, otherwise the tag or its base, any case), and the one the cursor names is called in its own instance; its answer is the page, the host setting `more`. A provider without an entry is an empty page. A shell wires it with `HostWiring::lookup()`.
