### Fixed

- Letter spacing, kerning and ligatures (Settings, Display) work again in the reader: the book is laid out and drawn with them, as in Beta 3. Kerning and ligatures are on by default, so lines break as they did in Beta 3. The font is asked for the pairs it kerns once for each book (about 7 KiB while it is open, 2 KiB more for a book with more than 96 different characters); if there is no room the reader goes on without the table, only slower. In a book of another script that starts in Latin, the letters after the first 96 are set without kerning, as before. Page counts made before are laid again once. The tables are let go while another screen covers the book (the store over a book) and made again on the next page.
