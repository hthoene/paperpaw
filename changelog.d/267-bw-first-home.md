### Changed

- **Home with covers (and the drawer, settings, Library covers and store pages) shows at once in black and white, its grays follow by themselves** (#267, Decision 196). Before, the device first drew the whole screen aside to count its pictures and then in grays, which took about 5 s on the X4 after "Back" from the book. Now the first frame is black and white with the covers dithered, and when the screen has pictures one gray picture follows after 3 seconds in which no key was pressed and nothing else was drawn; a key press begins the wait again (a focus step too), and nothing is drawn over a dialog. A screen that had its grays and draws itself again with no key since (Home each minute) keeps them. With "Bilder in Graustufen" off there is no gray picture. Book pages with pictures and the screen a device goes to sleep on keep their grays at once, as before.
