### Changed

- **Home editor: "+" and "Done" are buttons again** (#446). While you edit the home screen, "+" is a button with a frame and "Done" a filled button, as in Beta 3. On the X4 the Back key is named "Done" in the key bar while you edit (home 1.0.16, picture home 1.0.5).
- **Library: new books are marked, long titles take two lines** (#446). A book you have never opened shows a "New" chip at the end of its author line, as in Beta 3. A title that does not fit on one line takes a second one where the page has room left; the page keeps its five rows (files plugin 1.0.10).
- **Library on touch: the filter is a chip, the search a lens** (#446). On a touch screen the filter row is a chip ("All books ⌄") that opens a list of all filters with one tap, instead of stepping from one filter to the next; a lens beside it opens the search. The buttons keep the stepping row.
