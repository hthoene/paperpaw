### Added

- **Reader: the reading aids' rules for the native reader** (#257). The native reader can take what a text filter such as the reading aids answers to its `reader.filter` stage, check it once, and apply bold word beginnings, replaced words and the pictures rule to each block as Beta 3 did. It keeps only the answer's bytes (at most 3,840) while a book is open. The reading view uses it in a follow-up.
