### Fixed

- **Saved passwords that cannot be opened are told once, in a dialog** (#466). Passwords and keys saved on another device, or before the device's key was erased (a full re-flash), were named in a short message at every start. Now a dialog says it once, over the home screen: *OK* keeps them, *Remove them* removes just those entries (passwords that open here stay). The device keeps a small list of what it told (`.paperpaw/secrets-told.txt`, written only when it tells) and asks again only when a new one comes to the card. Entering such a password again where its plugin asks replaces the old entry as before. The emulator now treats such values as the X4 does, so it shows the dialog too.
