### Added

- **Native plugins hear the kernel's events** (#228). `core.tick` (3) and `started`, `resume`, `suspend`, `stopping`, `battery_measured` and `page_turned` (32 to 37) now reach the native plugins that subscribed to them. The kernel only queues them, in the same per-plugin queue as the setting events, so a plugin hears everything in one order; the pump tells them between callbacks, never inside one. Suspend and stopping are also told right after they are raised, from the kernel's top level, before the device sleeps or stops. Tick and resume coalesce into one pending bit each; the others are held within the event store's limits and, if they do not fit, dropped and logged.
