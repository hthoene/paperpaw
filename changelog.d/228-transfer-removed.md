### Changed

- **The interface-6 Wi-Fi transfer is gone: the native transfer is the only one** (#228). The plugin that served the web page,
  installed plugins from a computer, typed for the phone's keyboard and answered the USB cable is replaced by its
  interface-7 version, which does the first three. Until the host runs native plugins the *Wi-Fi transfer* entry, the
  keyboard's *Phone* key and the update page's *Wi-Fi transfer* button are not there. The USB cable transfer (the workshop's
  installs and books over the cable) and moving and renaming files from the web page wait for the cable stage, the
  dialogs and the library's move, and are not available until they land; move files on the device instead.
