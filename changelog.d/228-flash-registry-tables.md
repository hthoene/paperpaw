### Changed

- **The plugin list reads linked plugins' manifests from the program** (#248). Interface 7's one shape change for this (owner's Decision 168): `PluginInfo::manifest` is a `ManifestRef`, a manifest kept in the program (`Manifest::fixed`, `ManifestRef::fixed`) or a copy of its own, and it reads as a `Manifest` as before (`info.manifest.id`). `PluginInfo::new` takes either. A plugin's row in the list takes 40 bytes, not 104 (emulator).
- **Native actions keep their id and title in the program** (#248). Each linked plugin's build script makes a table of the id and title of every screen and action it declares (`paperpaw_sdk::native::declare::fixed_titles_source`, `NATIVE_TITLES`). The kernel keeps a native action whose id and title equal an entry as that entry (`paperpaw_sdk::Titled`), any other as a copy. `NativeAction::new` takes a `Titled`. An action's row takes 32 bytes, not 56 (emulator).
- **The kernel's reading setting groups are kept in the program** (#248). The ten parts of the reading settings and the space widget's group are `static`, as the kernel's other groups are, not made onto the heap at the start. About 0.9 KiB less (emulator).
- **Native screens keep their id and title in the program** (#248). The kernel's screens registry keeps interface-7 plugins' screens in a list of their own, each a `Titled` (a linked plugin's entry of `NATIVE_TITLES`, or a copy), apart from interface-6 plugins' screens. `ScreenList::new` takes that list too; `ScreenList::iter` still gives the screens plugins added in the order of their ids, then those kept in the program.
