### Added

- **Titles and authors of books in native formats, read in the background** (#228, #245). After a catalog walk, the host reads the title, first author, series and subjects of each book of a native format plugin whose details are still unread, one book at a time through its format plugin, and keeps them as the book's details; the next walk lists the book with them. It runs only after the start, when no book is open for reading and no cover job runs (the two share one background slot, its crash marker `.paperpaw/cover-job.txt` and its rules); opening a book or sleep ends it, a cover the library needs takes its place (the book is read later), and nothing holds the device awake: it goes on by timed wakes, 100 ms apart (1 s when saving power), and gives up a book after 10 minutes awake on it. A book the format fails is skipped until the next start. The catalog is walked again once when the job reaches the end of it (and at most every two minutes while it runs), only if a book was read.

### Changed

- **The background cover job is slower when saving power and gives up a book that never converts** (#245). Its steps are 1 s apart at the power levels Saving and Offline (100 ms otherwise), and a book whose cover jobs have kept the device awake 10 minutes in one start counts as failed (no cover until the next start), so a conversion that outlasts every awake session is not restarted after each wake for ever.
