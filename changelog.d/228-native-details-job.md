### Added

- **Titles and authors of books in native formats, read in the background** (#228, #245). After a catalog walk, the host reads the title, first author, series and subjects of each book of a native format plugin whose details are still unread, one book at a time through its format plugin, and keeps them as the book's details; the next walk lists the book with them. It runs only after the start, when no book is open for reading and no cover job runs (the two share one background slot and its crash marker `.paperpaw/cover-job.txt`); opening a book or sleep ends it, and nothing holds the device awake. A book the format fails is skipped until the next start. The catalog is walked again once when the job reaches the end of it (and at most every two minutes while it runs), only if a book was read.
