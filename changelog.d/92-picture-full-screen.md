### Added

- **Pictures full screen** (#92). Hold Select on a page with a picture (on a touch screen: hold the picture) to see it alone, fit to the whole screen. Left and Right step through the page's pictures, Select or Back return to the same page. The picture is not decoded again; the grays follow when the device has rested. Pages without a picture still bookmark on a hold of Select. Zooming into a picture and turning wide pictures need a larger decode and wait for the next interface (#110).
