### Added

- **Choose a setup when you set PaperPaw up** (#72). The setup guide has a new page after Wi-Fi: *Choose a setup* with ready-made PaperPaw templates — *Minimal*, *Reading statistics*, *Language learning* and *Notes* (English and German names and one-line descriptions) — and *Keep the defaults*. Choosing one applies its settings at once, switches on the built-in plugins it needs and fetches the others from the plugin store in the background (the plugins they require come with them). Without a connection the plugins wait, a notice says they will be installed when you are connected, and the store fetches them the next time it starts online or reaches the library. Plugin store 1.0.15; no interface change. Sharing your own setups comes later.
