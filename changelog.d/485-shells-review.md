### Fixed

- **Switching off with the power button held no longer drains the battery** (#485). A reader switched off while a case or a sleeve kept Power pressed waited at full speed until it was let go, for hours if need be. It now waits in light sleep.
- **USB drive: holding Power for 8 seconds switches off cleanly** (#485). The reader restarted instead (on the X4 Pro and the X4 Classic), and a hold while the drive was being prepared lent the card to the computer just before that restart. The drive also no longer ends by itself a few seconds after a computer wakes from standby.
- **A press while the sleep screen is redrawn is not replayed after waking** (#485). On the X4 and the X3, a button pressed during the redraw reached the reader only after the next wake, and Power could put it straight back to sleep. Power now wakes the reader there, the other buttons do nothing, as during the sleep.
- **X3 and Boox Picco: fixes the X4 already had** (#485). The X3 keeps the button readings out of the processor's sleep entry, as the X4 does. On the Picco, pulling the cable ends a doze on USB, and the processor sleeps between pages with the front light on.
- **Downloads and pages served by plugins** (#485). A full card now ends a download with the reason "full", not as an unknown error. A download redirected to https survives a lost network instead of failing only at its timeout. A page server no longer answers "busy" for good after the device ran short of memory, and an upload to a full card gets its error answer.
- **Wi-Fi: a known network is joined after the first scan** (#485). The Wi-Fi plugin decided from the networks of the scan before, so after switching on it saw none in reach until a second scan. It now sees the networks the scan found.
- **Wi-Fi: switched on in its settings, the networks in reach are listed** (#485). With no network joined before, switching Wi-Fi on did not look for networks, and *Networks* said none were found until *Look for networks again*.
- **X4 Pro and X4 Classic: two plugin pages can be served at once** (#485), as the plugin interface describes; the X4 and the X3 keep one.
- **Emulator: behaves as the Xteink devices do** (#485). Downloads show their progress; the Xteink profiles have the devices' network limits, do not join a network by themselves for a request, switch their radios off for a sleep, start anew after a switch-off, tell plugins when a card comes back, offer the USB link (and refuse the USB drive as a device without a cable does), and show plugin pages served in the device's own network at its address there.
