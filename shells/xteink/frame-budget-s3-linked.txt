# The linked plugins' own code in the S3 frame scan (todo 624, Senior #2892 M2). NOT included by the
# budgets yet: with it the scan finds a frame of 2,048 B or more in `NativeUpdate::event` (todo 625)
# and has to wait for it. Try it with `CHECK_S3_BUDGET_LINKED=1 tools/check-s3-budget.sh <worktree>`;
# once the cut is in, add `include frame-budget-s3-linked.txt` to both S3 budgets.
#
# Every callback of a linked plugin goes through `paperpaw_sdk::native::binding::entry`, as on the X4
# (`frame-budget-linked.txt`): rooting it follows what a plugin inlines into its handlers and calls
# directly. What the scan cannot follow (a closure, a `&dyn` call, a `match` jump table) is a call
# through a register; the callees of those are not scanned (device test S4). They are listed by crate,
# not by function: each line is a ceiling for the calls through a register in the functions of that
# crate (or of a prefix, `paperpaw_plugin_*`) that no `allow indirect` line of their own lists. The
# ceilings are what both devices measured at the head of PR #1447 plus 5 % and 2 (the two S3 builds
# differ by a call or two; a layout change of the build moves them as little). Above a ceiling is a
# new unfollowed call: read it, then raise the line with the reason.
