Yesterday afternoon the village watched two weary travellers wander towards the old tavern, where a waiter was wiping the tables after the lunch that Peter and Victor had taken together, to avoid the rain.

Water, wine and tea were served to every traveller who asked for them, and the warm yellow lamps were lit as soon as the afternoon turned towards a quiet and rather cold evening.

Wafers, waffles and fresh figs were offered after the late supper, and the very last of the visitors left towards midnight, waving to the tall waiter at the door of the tavern.

A fine official offer of fifty ﬁne flasks was affixed to the front of the office. Avery, Tobias and Wyatt typed the totals twice, to Valerie's visible relief, while the village waited for the final tally.
