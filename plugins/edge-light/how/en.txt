Swipe up or down along the edge of the screen to set the front light, like a slider: the light follows your finger in steps of 5 %, and a small notice shows the level. The left edge sets the brightness, the right edge the warmth. Raise your finger to stop.

The strip along each edge is a few millimetres wide. A tap there, and swipes anywhere else (pages, lists), work as always; so does a swipe sideways, also when it starts in the strip.

Under Edge light in the settings you choose what each edge sets (brightness, warmth or nothing) and how wide the strips are. Edge light is for devices with a touch screen and a front light; elsewhere it shows nothing.

Add the widget Front light to the home screen to see the level; tap it to switch the light off and on again at the level it had. The same is the action Front light: on or off, which you can put on a button or a menu.
