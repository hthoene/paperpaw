The sleep screen decides what the screen shows while the device sleeps. E-ink keeps the picture without power.

Choose it in the settings, under Device, Sleep screen, with Show: a sleeping cat (the default), the book's cover, my pictures, the logo and "Sleeping", widgets, the page as it is, or the page with a picture. My pictures shows the files of the Pictures folder one after the other.

Without a cover or a picture the cat shows. Where the system draws the sleep screen itself, these settings are hidden.

The off screen, Show when switched off, is drawn once as the device is switched off, from the power menu or after the time of Switch off after sleeping (not when a low battery cuts the power). It is as the sleep screen unless you choose the logo and "Switched off", the cat, the cover or my pictures. Another plugin can offer a screen for it.
