Simple home is a calm start screen for those who need only the core: a large clock, the book you are reading, the library and the store as big cards, and a dock of three icons at the bottom. It needs no setup.

Choose it in Settings, under the home screen. Its quick panel (choose it as the panel in Settings too) has big round switches, a bar for the front light where the device has one and a bar for the text size. With buttons, the arrow keys move the focus and Select does what the part says; on a bar it adds a tenth, and from the end goes back to the start. With touch, tap a part, or tap a bar where you want it.

The library lists your books as cards, the book opened last first, a page at a time, and opens the normal reader.

Make it yours in Settings, under Simple home: three lists you edit as you edit the main menu. Cards: which cards the home shows and in which order. Dock: which icons the dock holds, any action of any plugin. Quick panel: which switches and bars it shows. A card the screen has no room for is left out, from the end. Under Simple home you also choose whether the date and the battery show.
