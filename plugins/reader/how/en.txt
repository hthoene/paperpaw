The reader shows books page by page. Open a book from the library; the last book can reopen at start (setting Open last book at start).

Tap the left or right of the screen, or use the page buttons, to turn pages. Tap the middle or press Select for the reading menu: Contents, Search, Go to page, Font and layout, Bookmarks and highlights and more. Down from the top edge opens the quick panel, up from the bottom edge goes home; so does holding Back.

Hold Select (or hold a picture) to see a picture full screen: Left and Right step through the page's pictures. Select zooms to 2x, 3x and back (double tap on touch); zoomed, Left and Right pan sideways, the page buttons (or swipes) move on across and down. Back returns to fit, then to the page (hold to leave on touch). Tap the top right corner for a bookmark. Hold a word to highlight it. With buttons, hold Left: Left and Right move by word, the page buttons by line, holding them goes to the page's ends and by paragraph; Select opens the highlight bar for the word, holding Select widens it to the sentence and the paragraph (then Left and Right move the end, holding them the start). A selection goes on over page turns. Export as Markdown saves your notes.
