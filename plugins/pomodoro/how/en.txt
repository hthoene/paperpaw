A Pomodoro timer: work in focused rounds, then rest. It needs no setup: 25 minutes of focus, 5 of rest, and after four rounds a long break of 15.

Open Pomodoro from the tools menu and press Start, or add its widgets to the home screen (hold the home screen, then add a widget): Pomodoro timer shows the phase and the minutes left, Pomodoro start/pause is a button, and Pomodoro today counts the rounds you finished, with your daily goal. A tap on the timer starts or pauses it; hold it to open the full screen.

The timer keeps running when you leave the screen, and carries on by the clock after a restart. While the reader sleeps nothing runs: a phase that ended meanwhile is told when it wakes. A notice tells you when a phase is over. To save battery the clock updates once a minute (every ten seconds in the last minute on the full screen) and never keeps the reader awake.

Under Pomodoro in the settings you set the lengths and the rounds of a set, and whether the next phase starts by itself. Under More: a flash of the screen or a blink of the front light when a phase ends and a daily goal.
